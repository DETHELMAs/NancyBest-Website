About NancyBest Legacy

NancyBest Legacy Nigeria Limited is a registered professional writing agency and coaching organization incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission on January 28, 2025. Officially launched on April 25, 2025, the agency was built on one conviction: that writing is not a talent reserved for a few, it is a skill that can be learned, refined, and turned into a lasting livelihood.

The agency operates at the intersection of two worlds. On one side, it serves individuals, businesses, organizations, and thought leaders who need expert writing and communication services. On the other side, it runs structured training programmes across every major niche in the writing industry, raising a new generation of purposeful, commercially viable writers across Nigeria and Africa.

From its earliest days as a volunteer-powered idea to a growing agency with over 345 trained students across three cohorts, a 45-member team, a Board of Directors, and 17 courses taught by 17 specialist coaches, NancyBest Legacy has grown steadily because the mission has never changed: to make quality writing education accessible and to help every person find, refine, and deploy the power of their words.

02

The Founder's Story

NancyBest Ifeoma Lawrence did not stumble into writing. She arrived at it with intention. For years, she watched the online space fill up with creators offering everything to everyone. Courses on Social Media Management. Courses on copywriting. Courses on graphic design. Every niche imaginable, bundled together under the banner of digital skills. It was noisy, and in the noise, she found her answer. She would not do everything. She would do writing, and she would do it with depth.Writing had always been her constant. Even as she built skills across social media management, graphic design, and content strategy, writing was the thread that ran through all of it. So when the time came to build something of her own, she built it around that thread. She also saw a problem that others were not solving. The writing courses that existed outside were expensive, some costing as much as 45,000 to 50,000 naira for a single course. The people who needed those skills the most, the young Nigerians just stepping into the creative economy, the aspiring freelancers, the entrepreneurs building from nothing, could not afford them. NancyBest Legacy was her answer to that gap. Quality writing education at a price that does not shut people out.

She started the registration process in January 2025. By March of that same year, she was already gathering her first volunteers, people who believed in what she was building before there was much to show. By April 25, 2025, the agency launched with a five-day writing competition that drew 30 contestants and 3 judges. It was that competition that announced to the world who NancyBest Legacy was and what it had come to do. Three cohorts and 345 students later, the vision has only grown wider. A Board of Directors was onboarded in early 2026. A Teens Mentorship Programme launched in January 2026. Seventeen courses now run simultaneously, each taught by a specialist coach. And the agency's client- facing writing services continue to serve businesses, nonprofits, and professionals who need words that work.

NancyBest Lawrence is a wife, a mother, a content strategist, a ghostwriter, and a builder. What she is building at NancyBest Legacy is not just a company. It is a legacy that will outlive her, carried forward by every writer she has trained, every professional she has served, and every young person who found their voice through a programme that believed they were worth investing in.

Social Media Content Creation

NancyBest Legacy did not arrive fully formed. It was assembled piece by piece, person by person, each step building on the one before it.


January 28, 2025 Corporate Affairs Commission registration completed. NancyBest Legacy Nigeria Limited became an official legal entity.
March 11, 2025 Operations began with four founding volunteers: Christopher Ayodele, Grace Amarachi, Zainab Annes, and Glorious. Over the following weeks, new volunteers joined one by one until the team reached eight members by March 22. On March 25, roles and responsibilities were formally assigned.
March 31, 2025 The brand went live on Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp, entering the public space with a clear identity and a clear purpose.
April 21 to April 25, 2025 — The Launch NancyBest Legacy officially launched with a five-day writing competition featuring 30 contestants and 3 judges. It was this competition that publicly announced the agency's arrival and what it had come to do.
May 2025 The Graphic Design team lead, Divine Obiajulu Okeke, joined on May 1. Claire Metuh and Rebecca Afolabi joined on May 6, expanding the team to 10 active volunteers.
July 20, 2025 Twelve new volunteers were onboarded in a single day, bringing the total team to 25 members across content writing, social media management, graphic design, and administration.
October 2025 — Cohort 1 The first writing training cohort launched with 10 courses across different sub-niches in writing. Students paid a token amount to attend and a small certification fee. A total of 146 students enrolled.
Cohort 2 The programme ran again with 10 courses. The certification fee increased to 2,000 naira, with 1,000 naira going to coaches and 1,000 naira retained by NancyBest Legacy. Total enrollment reached 103 students. Coaches were paid based on the number of students in their individual courses.
Cohort 3 The course catalogue expanded to 17 sub-niches, each taught by a dedicated specialist coach. The certification fee moved to 3,000 naira. Coach compensation was restructured to a flat-rate model, distributing payment equitably regardless of individual course enrollment. By this cohort, cumulative total student enrollment across all three cohorts reached 345.
January 2026 The NancyBest Legacy Teens Mentorship Programme launched, marking the agency's first formal programme specifically designed for young people. This represented a significant expansion of the brand's educational mandate.
February to March 2026 A Board of Directors was formally onboarded, comprising 10 individuals who serve simultaneously as brand ambassadors. This leadership structure marked NancyBest Legacy's transition into a more structured organizational phase.

Volunteer Structure & Benefits

Volunteers are not peripheral to NancyBest Legacy. They are the engine behind the brand's daily operations, its creative output, and its growing community. The agency takes the volunteer experience seriously, structuring it with clear expectations, meaningful compensation, and genuine pathways for growth.

Volunteer Benefits

  1. Learning and Development: Volunteers receive a 50% discount on all courses offered by the agency, along with access to selected trainings, mentorship sessions, and learning resources.
  2. Hands-On Experience: Volunteers gain practical, real-world experience through active involvement in team projects, organizational operations, and client-adjacent work.
  3. CV Support: Free professional CV writing support is provided to every volunteer to strengthen their career profile.
  4. Certificate of Service: Volunteers who complete a minimum of one month of service receive an official Certificate of Service in recognition of their contribution.
  5. Letter of Recommendation: Volunteers who demonstrate commitment and responsibility are eligible for a formal Letter of Recommendation upon request.
  6. Recognition and Awards: Outstanding volunteers receive an Award of Excellence and may be featured on official platforms including social media and at events.
  7. Growth and Promotion Pathways: High-performing volunteers can be promoted from Team Member to Team Lead Assistant, and from Team Lead Assistant to Team Lead. Cross-department transfers are also available based on skills and fit.
  8. Formal Send-Off: Volunteers who complete their service or depart properly receive a formal send-off in appreciation of their contribution.

Volunteer Core Values

Every volunteer represents the spirit, standards, and reputation of the NancyBest Legacy brand. These eight values guide how volunteers serve, learn, and contribute within the NBL community:

  • Service With Purpose
  • Commitment to Excellence
  • Continuous Learning
  • Integrity and Accountability
  • Collaboration and Community
  • Empowerment Through Knowledge
  • Respect and Professional Conduct
  • Impact-Driven Service

The Road Ahead

NancyBest Legacy was birthed with one mission in mind: to raise purpose- driven individuals through the power of words, service, and intentional growth. Everything built so far, the cohorts, the coaches, the volunteer teams, the board, has been foundation work. The structure now being put in place is designed to carry a much larger vision. This is where we are going.


The Writer's Arsenal

A certified online and physical training institute where aspiring writers, creators, entrepreneurs, and individuals across Africa and beyond can access structured training in every major writing discipline. This institute will offer over 20 specialist courses, each taught by experienced coaches and mentors, with real certification that carries professional weight.


Legacy Internship Programme

A structured, hands-on internship opportunity designed for young creatives who need more than theory. Participants will build real skills, gain verifiable experience, and work on live projects that challenge and grow them as professionals.


Publishing and Media Unit

A publishing arm that helps writers publish and distribute their books, ebooks, and audio content across platforms, at affordable rates and with professional support, removing the barrier that keeps talented Nigerian writers from reaching global audiences.


Annual Legacy Conference

A gathering that brings together writers, creatives, thought leaders, and changemakers to learn, network, and collaborate on purpose-driven ideas. The conference will serve as an annual landmark in Nigeria's writing and creative economy calendar.


Local Chapters and Campus Ambassadors

Legacy Clubs will be established across primary schools, secondary schools, and universities, empowering young people at the grassroots level and creating a generational ripple effect of growth, literacy, and creative leadership.


Legacy Fund and Scholarships

A fund that supports talented individuals who cannot afford creative training, giving them access to resources and mentorship that can change the direction of their lives. Writing opened doors for those who founded this agency; it should open doors for every person willing to walk through them.


NancyBest Legacy Teens Mentorship Programme

Launched in January 2026, this programme marks the agency's formal commitment to young people below the age of 18. The Teens Programme provides structured mentorship, age-appropriate writing education, and guided exposure to creative careers for teenagers across Nigeria. It is built on the belief that the earlier a young person finds their voice through writing, the further they will go.


The programme launched its first cohort this year and represents one of the agency's most meaningful milestones, proof that NancyBest Legacy is not building for today's writers alone, but for the generation that is still growing into their words. This is more than a vision. It is a roadmap.


NancyBest Legacy is not just about now. It is about the generations to come. Together, we are not just writing, we are building bridges, transforming minds, and creating legacies that last. And if we stay rooted in service, excellence, and purpose, nothing can stop us.
